# Pocket Director AI — SEO Strategy
**Prepared:** March 2026 | **Role:** Marketing Director
**Domain:** pocketdirectorai.com | **Stage:** Early-stage SaaS, ~100+ brand customers

---

## Executive Summary

Pocket Director AI sits at the intersection of two explosive search trends: **AI-generated content** and **e-commerce product photography**. The site currently has zero blog content, no meta descriptions, and no structured data — meaning 100% of traffic is paid or direct. This strategy unlocks organic as a primary growth channel within 90 days.

**Projected organic opportunity:**
- 12-month target: 8,000–15,000 monthly organic visitors
- Primary conversion: Free trial signups (200 credits, no CC required)
- Estimated CAC reduction vs. paid: ~60–70%

### Current Title Tag
> "Pocket Director AI - Create Cinematic Product Videos in Minutes"

**Issue:** Misses primary keyword "AI product photography" entirely. Video is a secondary feature.

### Critical Gaps
1. **No blog** = no content to rank, no topical authority, no backlink-worthy assets
2. **No meta description** = Google writes one from random page content (CTR killer)
3. **No schema** = missing FAQ rich snippets, star ratings in SERPs
4. **No comparison/alternative pages** = losing high-intent "vs" and "alternative" searches
5. **Single-page site** = every SEO keyword competes for one URL

## 6. Link Building Strategy

### Tier A — Easy Wins (First 30 Days)
These require minimal effort and build foundational authority:

1. **Product Directories:** Submit to Product Hunt, G2, Capterra, AlternativeTo, Futurepedia, There's An AI For That, TopAI.tools
2. **Affiliate Partners:** Activate your existing affiliate program for backlinks from partner sites
3. **Stripe Climate:** Request a backlink from the Stripe Climate partners page (you're already contributing 1%)
4. **Crunchbase / AngelList:** Create company profiles (DA 90+, free backlinks)

### Tier B — Content-Driven Links (Month 2–3)
5. **Data Study:** Publish "The True Cost of Product Photography in 2026" with original survey data — pitch to e-commerce blogs (Practical Ecommerce, Shopify Blog contributors)
6. **"Best AI Tools" Roundups:** Pitch inclusion in "best AI photography tools" and "best AI tools for e-commerce" listicles
7. **Guest Posts:** Target Shopify Partners Blog, A2X Blog, Jungle Scout Blog, Helium 10 Blog — they all have large Amazon/Shopify audiences
8. **YouTube Tutorials:** Create "How I made product photos with AI" videos — rank on YouTube + earn embed backlinks

### Tier C — Authority Links (Month 3+)
9. **PR Play:** Use the "$35,640/year savings" stat as a media hook — pitch to TechCrunch, Forbes, Entrepreneur via HARO/Qwoted
10. **Case Studies:** Turn Sarah Chen's "35% conversion rate increase" into a full case study — pitch to CRO and e-commerce publications
11. **Podcast Appearances:** Pitch the founder to e-commerce podcasts (My Wife Quit Her Job, 2X eCommerce, Shopify Masters)
