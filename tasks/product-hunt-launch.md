## Assets to Prepare

### Thumbnail (240x240px)
- Logo on clean white or indigo background
- No text (thumbnail is too small)

### Gallery images (1270x952px, upload 5–6):
1. Before/after: phone photo → 8 professional AI images
2. Amazon-compliant white background example
3. Lifestyle scene example
4. UGC video frame example
5. Pricing/value comparison ($300–$800 per product with photographer vs $15.20/month)
6. The 3-step workflow: Upload → Select style → Download

### Demo video (optional but boosts ranking):
- 60–90 second screen recording of the actual product workflow
- Show: upload product photo → select style → images generate → download
- Add simple captions, no voiceover needed
- Export as MP4, upload to YouTube first then embed
